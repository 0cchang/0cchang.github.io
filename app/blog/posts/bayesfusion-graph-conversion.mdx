This one was a fun project because I was able to apply a basic graph algorithm into real life, at the time I just got into college, and I wasn't even in the CS program yet. 
I knew that I was behind many applicants, and cold emailed many professors to see if I could get any project. One of them finally responded and it was in the Civil and Environmental engineering program.
I met with her in the office and she showed me some problems I could work from. 

This project, involved converting data structures from being modeled in one software to another. Specifically fault trees which was the original strucure, to bayesian graphs.
I had no real background in Civil Engineering and learned about the business requirements from one of her PHD students. 

I created the parser to read the original fault tree files, then implemented a DFS algorithm to map them in java, then from the object I would DFS again to output the correct bayesian grpah.

I think nowadays this project would be done in 2 prompts, but doing it all by hand especially as a complete newbie helped me alot. 
I would encourage new students to email professors and their PHD students, at least one of them would be supportive enough to bring you in.
