For CMSC 848U, I built automated pipelines to extract precise ear geometry from Meta and HUTUBS head-scan point-cloud datasets. I then developed testing metrics to evaluate head-related transfer functions in 3D audio environments with Unity and Steam Audio.

I will add experiments, visualizations, and conclusions here as the project develops.
