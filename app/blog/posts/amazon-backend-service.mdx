At Amazon in Seattle, I expanded a backend service with AWS Lambda, SQS, and S3 to support more than 1,000 daily transactions. I also added metrics, alarms, and logging so the service could be operated confidently after launch.

I will use this post as a running technical account of the architecture, failure modes, and the runbooks that made on-call work safer.
